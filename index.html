3NF Relational Tables & Data Dictionary

10 Normalized Entities
1. branches

PK: branch_id • Stores physical rental pickup/dropoff stations, operating hours, and dispatch coordinates.

2. users & customer_profiles

PK: user_id, customer_id • Handles authentication, driver's license KYC validation, and loyalty tier rankings.

3. vehicle_categories

PK: category_id • Defines pricing tiers (EV, Luxury, SUV, Sports), base rates, and driver age eligibility.

4. vehicles

PK: vehicle_id • FKs to category and branch. Tracks VIN, plate, mileage, fuel type, rates, and operational status.

5. bookings (Central Fact Entity)

PK: booking_id • Binds customer, vehicle, branches, dates, rates, taxes, and status in full 3NF conformity.

6. rental_agreements & payments

PK: agreement_id, payment_id • Digital contract agreement tokens, transaction references, invoices, and deposit holds.
